My Background

My name is Hoda Pady and I'm from Yemen. My family and I moved to New York in January 2013, and one of the first things I remember is waking up in a hotel room to a bright, sunny morning. When I opened the curtains, I was completely taken aback by the amount of snow outside. The sunlight reflecting off the snow was so bright that it was blinding. I had never experienced snow in real life before, so later stepping outside and seeing and smelling the freshly fallen snow felt so magical. That morning has stayed with me ever since and is one of my favorite memories from when my family first moved to New York.

My Hobbies

I love to read, play chess, and watch Formula 1 on the weekends. I also really enjoy learning languages. I currently speak Turkish, Arabic, and English, and I'm in the process of learning Japanese, French, and Italian.

I do not like losing at chess, although I'm slowly learning that it is part of the game. Every loss is an opportunity to learn from my mistakes and improve my game, even if I would much rather win.
